# Before Quiz
- Arrive early and log into a desktop. No need to open any app or browser.
- Put all your items inside your bag and send the bag at the front of the lab
    room. You may keep pen or pencil with you, but not any paper or resource.
- Check your pocket and nearby your desk to ensure no phone or paper or
    resource is present.

# Beginning Quiz
- Unlock the desktop and enter your ID and programming language information
    into the console window.
- Wait for some time, and finally ensure that the VS Code window opens and in
    the left panel at least some file names containing your ID is present.
- Find the tasks inside folders named like "_TID" (the task ID, an underscore
    followed by 3 alphanumeric characters). The whole system in such a folder
    is called a checker. Find checker related instructions inside the file
    "_TID_Instruction.md".
- Check if Autosave in VS Code is turned on. If not turned on then turn it on.

# During Quiz
- Do not keep any mobile phones or resource nearby.
- Do not use any internet or cloud service.
- Do not keep or open any web browser.
- Do not open or view or copy any pre-written code.
- Do not open or use any AI/GPT/AutoCoding/Chatting service.

# Ending Quiz
- Do not run the checker near the end of the quiz.
- Ensure that your code is saved before the end of the quiz.

# After Quiz
- Do not leave the lab before ensuring your submission and attendance.
